Visual Direction

Game-first, less dashboard, more library.

This mockup reduces clip-card clutter and lets the user think in games first. The games rail acts like a quick jump surface, while each card keeps only the actions that matter most in the moment: open and share.

Why it feels cleaner The card no longer carries rename, delete, edit, and highlight controls all at once. Those move into overflow or secondary affordances.
Why it organizes better The user can jump to a game directly instead of manipulating filters and then scanning a long undifferentiated list of clips.
Why it fits your current flow Open goes to the native player for smooth viewing. Share still routes through the in-app player where your identification and overlay logic adds value.
